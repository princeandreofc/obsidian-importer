Some voices you recognise in a single bar.

London's Ali Love — frontman, songwriter, entertainer — has spent two decades lending his to the records that shaped UK dance music: the Chemical Brothers, Justice, CamelPhat, Róisín Murphy. Always unmistakably himself, whoever's name was on the sleeve.

The club voice, at symphony scale.

Twenty-plus years in, hundreds of thousands listening every month, Ali Love is recording a new solo album. The voice is not done. It is just getting warmed up.

Press · Quiet proof

Club records with a soul singer inside them.

With Infinity Ink and Hot Natured, Ali helped define an era. “Forward Motion” and the benchmark “Benediction” — written with Jamie Jones and Lee Foss — remain reference points of psychedelic house. His “Confusion” with Keinemusik became an Essential New Tune for Pete Tong on BBC Radio 1 — and was covered by Drake, who cites the song as an inspiration.

“Rarely less than entertaining.”Resident Advisor
